Method, assumptions and limits

  • Automodel. Towers by DBSCAN on classified points; wires separated per span and fitted with a robust least-squares catenary z = z₀ + a(cosh((x−x₀)/a) − 1).
  • Environments. H = a·w from the survey fit; change-of-state equation over the ruling span; wind swing φ = atan(P·d / w) about the chord between attachments. Ice adds ρgπt(d+t) per metre and widens the wind area to d+2t. At suspension towers the insulator string swings by ψ = atan(qL / (wL + Wins/2)), moving the attachment; strain ends and earth wires do not swing.
  • Clearance. Vegetation: 3D distance. Buildings: vertical clearance above parts under the line and horizontal clearance beside it (CEA Reg. 63); a part fails only when it is inside both. Ground: vertical. Roads, railways and rivers: vertical, where the surface lies under the wire, against their own crossing values. Rules by voltage class (preset “generic_demo”); voltage from the GIS line. SpanGuard demo table. Not a regulatory standard: replace with the utility's own rules.
  • Conductors (assumed). AAAC ~570 mm² (d 31.1 mm, 1.57 kg/m, EA 31.35 MN); Earth wire ~95/55 (d 16.0 mm, 0.707 kg/m, EA 16.35 MN). Survey conductor temperature: 15.0 °C, fixed config value (assumed). The hot case is checked at each conductor's maximum operating temperature.
  • Current rating. IEEE Std 738-2012 steady-state heat balance qc + qr = qs + I²R(T). The span's rating temperature is the lower of the conductor's thermal limit and the clearance-limited temperature, then converted to amps for the rating weather (35.0 °C air, 0.61 m/s wind at 90.0°, 1000.0 W/m² sun). Validated on the IEEE 738 Drake example (1025 A at 100 °C, reproduced within 0.1%).
  • Uncertainty. Monte Carlo varies survey temperature and conductor data; grow-in assumes uniform vertical regrowth.
  • Limits. Edge spans (far tower outside the survey) are reported at survey conditions only. No creep or ACSR two-material model; ruling-span (not finite-element) sag-tension. Insulator lengths and weight are assumed by voltage class. Trees fall about their base as rigid poles. Unless the CEA preset is selected, clearance values are demo values and must be replaced by the utility's standard.
